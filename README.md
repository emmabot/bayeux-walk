# The Bayeux Tapestry: a 3D walk

Walk the whole Bayeux Tapestry in your browser, hung at true scale on a gallery wall.

**Live:** https://emmabot.github.io/bayeux-walk/

- Enter with a winning ticket, then drag, scroll or use the arrow keys to walk.
- Real Latin inscriptions with translations and notes for 30 sections.
- A spot-it hunt for ten details, a map of 1066, a running tally and a gift-shop exit.

## Image credit

The tapestry imagery is the 2017 high-resolution photography by the Université de Caen Normandie, CNRS and ENSICAEN, published on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:2017_high-resolution_photography_of_the_Bayeux_Tapestry) as public domain. The 56 scene photographs were aligned, joined and cropped to the cloth for this site.

The Bayeux Tapestry is on loan to the [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry) from 10 September 2026 to 11 July 2027. Go and see the real thing if you can.
